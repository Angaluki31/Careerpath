# PathFinder

PathFinder helps people find their way into tech, or into a different part of it. You tell it about your interests and professional background, and it suggests the career path that suits you best, then gives you a roadmap for following it.

## Who it's for

- **Beginners** who are curious about tech but don't know where to start
- **Career switchers** coming from other fields who want to move into tech
- **People already in tech** who want to transition to a different role

## How it works

1. Answer questions about your interests, skills and work history
2. Get matched with the tech paths that fit you best
3. Follow a step-by-step roadmap for the path you choose
